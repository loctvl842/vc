notify-toast

Plain information messages show as a quiet one-line toast at the bottom right (BeastVim's toast style); repeats merge into a count. Warnings and errors keep the cards at the top right.

Problem

Every message is a boxed card in the top right. For an error that is right; for "Hit BOTTOM, continuing at TOP" it is noise covering the finder, and five repeats stack five identical big cards. Information and problems look equally heavy.

Why now: messages are the only feedback and appear often; a light style for routine ones lets errors and warnings stand out.

Toast details
  • One line, no border or box; the terminal background shows through.
  • Dim text; the icon at the right end carries the level color.
  • Width follows the text, between 10 columns and 60% of the terminal.
  • Fades in and out in place (no slide). Stacks upward from the bottom right, newest at the bottom.

Scenarios

1 - A routine message

  1. The user searches past the end of the file in the preview. A toast fades in bottom right: "Hit BOTTOM, continuing at TOP". Nothing top right.
  2. The user keeps typing; the toast never takes focus.
  3. About 2 seconds later it fades out.

2 - An error and a toast at once

  1. The user presses x in the preview (error) right after a search wrap (info). E21 shows as a card top right; the search wrap as a toast bottom right. Each expires on its own timing.

3 - The same message repeats

  1. A message appears once: "written".
  2. Same message again while visible: the line becomes "written x2", no second line, timer restarts.
  3. The count keeps going: x3 ... x99, then "x99+".

4 - Different messages

  1. Three different messages within a second: three lines appear one after another, staggered, newest at the bottom.
  2. They fade out oldest first; the rest drop down to close the gap.

5 - Long and multi-line message

  1. A multi-line message shows on one line, breaks turned into spaces.
  2. Wider than 60% of the terminal: cut with an ellipsis; the icon stays visible.

6 - Small terminal

  1. 40 columns wide, an information message arrives: the toast is at most 24 columns (60%), text cut with an ellipsis.

7 - Warning stays a card

  1. A warning arrives (e.g. "claude was not found on PATH"): it shows as a WARN card top right, never as a toast.

Behavior rules

  • Information goes to a toast; warnings and errors go to cards. Debug and trace are still not shown.
  • Covers messages Neovim prints and any other information-level notice in the editor.
  • Identical information toasts still on screen merge into one line with a count (x2 to x99, then x99+); each merge restarts the timer.
  • Messages that differ in text never merge.
  • Toasts stay about 2 seconds; cards about 3 seconds, as before.
  • Always one line: line breaks become spaces, long text ends with an ellipsis, the count is never the part that gets cut.
  • Toasts never take focus or steal keys.
  • Toasts sit in the very last screen row, right aligned with a one column margin, above everything else.
  • Colors follow the terminal palette and update on light/dark switch. No fixed colors.
  • No flicker or redraw of the finder.
  • Many toasts in quick succession are staggered a little.
  • Background messages show safely and never cause an error themselves.
  • If the toast stack would grow past the top of the screen, the oldest are removed early.

Success criteria

  • An information message is one dim line at the bottom right, no box.
  • A warning or error is a card at the top right, as before.
  • Five identical information messages in a row show one line with "x5".
  • Multi-line messages show on one line; long ones end with an ellipsis.
  • Colored icon at the right end; follows the terminal palette in light and dark.
  • Toasts fade in and out and the stack closes gaps.
  • Typing continues uninterrupted while toasts are visible.
  • A 40-column terminal shows a readable, shortened toast.
  • A background message shows as a toast with no error.
  • "Press ENTER or type command to continue" still never appears.

Out of scope

  • Progress bars and spinners (BeastVim's toast has them; VimClaude reports no progress).
  • Warnings or errors as toasts, or information as cards: the split is fixed.
  • A message history window.
  • A key to dismiss toasts at once.
  • Per-message style choices.
